## Hershey fonts

`tools/intro/cursive.json` contains glyph data of the Hershey "cursive" font, originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey at the U.S. National Bureau of Standards. The Hershey font data is generally regarded as being in the public domain.

The commonly distributed version of the data carries the following notice:

> The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at the U. S. National Bureau of Standards.
> The format of the Font data in this distribution was originally created by James Hurt, Cognition, Inc., 900 Technology Park Drive, Billerica, MA 01821.

Some glyphs are modified for readability (`tools/intro/readable.json`).
